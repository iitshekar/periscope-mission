Learning objectives

  • State the law of reflection: angle of incidence = angle of reflection.
  • Measure angles from the normal (a line at 90° to the mirror).
  • Explain why periscope mirrors are set at 45° and parallel.
  • Describe the image seen through a periscope.

Key vocabulary

  • Incident ray – the light ray arriving at the mirror.
  • Reflected ray – the light ray leaving the mirror.
  • Normal – imaginary line at 90° to the mirror surface.
  • Angle of incidence (i) – between incident ray and normal.
  • Angle of reflection (r) – between reflected ray and normal.
